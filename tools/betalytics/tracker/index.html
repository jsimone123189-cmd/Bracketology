Picks

Picks are the Betalytics model's side for each market, priced at the last run before tip-off. Graded against ESPN final scores, flat 1 unit risked per pick. "Model %" is the average hit chance Betalytics gave the graded picks.

Signal compares the units to what pure luck would produce at the same odds (a z-score). Under 100 decided picks it reads "Too few". "Likely edge" or "Likely losing" means the result is more than 2 standard deviations from luck. Even then, check that it holds as the sample grows: with many groups, one will look strong by chance. A realistic edge (54–55% at −110) usually takes 1,000+ picks to show.
